Retinol Tips: Skin Benefits and How to Maximize Its Effects

Published on Aug 19, 2022

HISTORY OF VITAMIN A

Retinoids were initially used as a topical and oral medication for a number of skin conditions, most notably acne. After years of close observation, doctors and scientists have discovered that Retinoids have the added benefit of repairing and rejuvenating sun-damaged skin. They help improve overall skin texture and reverse signs of aging.

SO HOW DO THEY WORK?

Our skin cells have a receptor for Retinoic Acid. Retinoic Acid acts as a wake-up call for our skin cells to do their job properly. Retinoids help to increase the rate of skin cell turnover and help to fundamentally renew our skin cells while increasing the production of collagen, the building block of our skin, and also helps to eliminate hyperpigmentation.

WHO CAN USE IT?

Anyone who has signs of aging such as fine lines, wrinkles, loss of elasticity, acne problems and wants to prevent premature aging can benefit from using Retinoids. You may be wondering when is the right time to start. There is no clear, definitive answer to this. However, if your main problem is acne, you can start using it early in adolescence (around the age of 15-17) by consulting your doctor. Additionally, the truth is that by the age of 25, our skin collagen production decreases significantly by 1% each year. This fact is a good reason to start your Retinoid journey when you reach your mid-to-late 20s. Using this ingredient carefully will help prevent your skin from developing fine lines and wrinkles early on!
