Why does skin age faster in older ages?

Published on Jan 2, 2024

Why does skin age faster in older ages?

With age, cellular renewal in the skin slows down, collagen and elastin levels decrease. Over time, these changes lead to loosening, wrinkles and sagging. The speed of aging is affected by many factors such as genetics, lifestyle and environmental factors.

How to Prevent Skin Aging?

Skin aging is a natural process, but with the right steps it is possible to slow down this process and achieve a healthy, youthful appearance. Genetic factors, environmental influences and lifestyle habits determine the speed of aging.

The main methods to be considered to prevent skin aging:

The sun’s harmful UV rays are one of the biggest causes of premature aging. Using a broad-spectrum sunscreen daily can help protect your skin from photoaging and prevent wrinkles from forming.

  • Dry skin is one of the factors that accelerate aging. You can balance the moisture of your skin by using moisturizing creams and products containing hyaluronic acid. Drinking plenty of water also supports skin health.
  • Antioxidants prevent cell damage caused by free radicals. Ingredients such as vitamin C, vitamin E, and green tea protect the skin from external factors and reduce the signs of aging.
  • Anti-aging ingredients such as retinol, peptides and niacinamide increase skin elasticity and reduce the appearance of wrinkles. With regular use, they accelerate the skin renewal process.
  • Stress can cause the release of hormones that trigger skin aging. Managing stress with methods such as yoga, meditation and breathing exercises are effective in maintaining skin health. It is important to create a regular care routine and adopt a healthy lifestyle to prevent skin aging. Taking care of your skin is the most effective way to defy time..
  • Be Patient and Observe Results: Skin responds differently to new products. Monitor the effects for at least a few weeks before evaluating its suitability. Patience is crucial for seeing results.
